What did your school score on the state report card?

About the data

Source: Wisconsin Department of Public Instruction, 2025-26 school report card data file, released Nov. 17, 2026. Schools listed are those DPI lists with the city of Milwaukee. Fox Point-Bayside schools, which have a Milwaukee mailing address, are not included.

Proficient means scoring Meeting or Advanced on state tests (Forward Exam, PreACT Secure, ACT or Dynamic Learning Maps). State figures cover all Wisconsin students in the same tested grades.

Growth compares how much students' test scores went up in a year with students statewide who started at similar levels. Each school is ranked against Wisconsin schools that serve the same grades.

  • MPS schools: the overall ranking comes from the school's DPI report card PDF.
  • Charter and choice schools: NNS calculated the overall ranking from DPI's growth scores. Tested on MPS schools, this method matched DPI's rankings within half a point.
  • Reading and math rankings: calculated by NNS for all schools. DPI does not publish them.
  • Schools with an alternate rating or no rating do not get a growth ranking.

Points = DPI's score for each area times the weight DPI assigns it.

Chronic absenteeism is the share of students who missed more than 10% of school days, averaged over recent years. It is calculated from DPI's chronic absenteeism score (100 minus the score).
